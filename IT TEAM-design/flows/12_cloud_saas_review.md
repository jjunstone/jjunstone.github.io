Cloud/SaaS Adoption Review Flow

Trigger: Business unit requests cloud/SaaS service

Steps:
1. Business unit submits SaaS/Cloud adoption request
2. Infra Staff gathers requirements (data volume, sensitivity, location)
3. Security Lead reviews data sensitivity and regulations
4. DBA Lead reviews data residency requirements (Article 21-2)
5. Network Manager reviews network integration needs
6. Infra Staff prepares security assessment
7. DBA Lead performs vendor security audit
8. Legal/Compliance reviews contract terms
9. DBA Lead approves or rejects
10. If approved: Infra Staff manages onboarding
11. Quarterly review of cloud services

Approvals:
- Security Lead (security controls)
- DBA Lead (final authority)

Verifications:
- Data residency within approved geography
- Encryption in transit/at rest
- Access controls
- Audit logging capability

Audit Log:
- Service name, vendor
- Data classification
- Review date
- Approval/rejection reason
- Contract terms
- Renewal dates

Regulations:
- Electronic Finance Act Article 21-2 (Cloud)
- ISO 27001 A.14.2 (Supplier management)
