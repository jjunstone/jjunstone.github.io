External Audit Response Flow

Trigger: External audit scheduled (annual, compliance)

Steps:
1. DBA Lead receives audit notification
2. DBA Lead coordinates with all teams for document preparation
3. Teams gather evidence (logs, configurations, policies)
4. Security Lead prepares compliance matrix
5. DBA Lead conducts internal pre-audit
6. Auditor reviews systems and processes
7. Teams respond to auditor questions
8. DBA Lead documents findings and remediation plan
9. Teams execute remediation actions
10. DBA Lead verifies closure

Approvals:
- DBA Lead (final authority on audit response)

Audit Log:
- Audit date and scope
- Finding IDs and severity
- Remediation plan
- Closure date

Regulations:
- ISMS-P certification audit (annual)
- ISO 27001 certification audit
