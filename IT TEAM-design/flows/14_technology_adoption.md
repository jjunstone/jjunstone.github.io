Technology Adoption Review Flow

Trigger: Proposal for new software, infrastructure component, or service

Steps:
1. Team proposes new technology
2. Proposer submits requirements and justification
3. DBA Lead reviews operational fit
4. Security Lead reviews security implications
5. Network Manager reviews network impact (if applicable)
6. Infra Staff reviews cost and licensing
7. DBA Lead performs vendor security review
8. DBA Lead makes approval decision
9. If approved: procurement and deployment planning
10. Post-deployment: operational handoff and training

Approvals:
- DBA Lead (final authority)

Considerations:
- Operational complexity for 10-person team
- Security posture impact
- Compatibility with existing stack
- Cost and licensing compliance
- Vendor lock-in risk
- Support and maintenance burden

Audit Log:
- Proposal date
- Technology name and vendor
- Decision and rationale
- Implementation date
