General Incident Response Flow

Trigger: System outage or service degradation

Steps:
1. Monitoring alerts on-call engineer
2. Engineer assesses impact (affected systems, users, duration estimate)
3. Engineer attempts quick fix
4. If unsuccessful: escalate to team lead
5. Team lead coordinates response across teams
6. Implement remediation
7. Verify service restoration
8. Post-incident: root cause analysis
9. Implement preventive measures

Approvals:
- Team lead (escalation, major changes)

Audit Log:
- Alert timestamp
- Detection-to-response time
- Root cause
- Resolution time (MTTR)
- Post-incident actions
